The 6-Week Journey

A scaffolded programme from preparation to performance — designed for intentional growth at every stage.

Weeks 1–2

Identity, Clarity & Targeting

"Doubt turns into direction."
  • Understand what you stand for and what you bring
  • Build your Unique Value Proposition (UVP)
  • Craft language that helps others remember you
  • Position yourself with greater confidence
Weeks 3–4

Simulation & High-Pressure Rehearsal

"Preparation turns into confidence."
  • Test your thinking in real-world scenarios
  • Refine your communication language
  • Learn to stay steady during important moments
  • Build presence and control in key conversations
Weeks 5–6

Reconnection Strategy & Intention

"Confidence turns into momentum."
  • Master follow-up techniques that work
  • Stay memorable long after the conversation
  • Build momentum through sustained effort
  • Create your personal growth plan beyond the programme
The Framework

7 Seconds to Make an Impression

First impressions happen fast. This programme teaches you how to use those seven seconds to show your best self — every time.

F

FEEL

What emotion do you create in others the moment you speak? Warmth, energy, positivity — this is what people remember first.

T

THINK

What do people conclude about you? Confident. Purposeful. Focused on others. Shape the narrative from the first sentence.

H

HOOK

What makes them want to know more? Your hook is the one thing that makes listeners lean in and ask the next question.

Example — DrShika's Opening

"Hi, I'm Darshika. I help people shine their light and connect with confidence so they can open doors to new opportunities."

Meet Darshika Patel

Darshika Patel is a career coach, speaker, and the founder of DrShika Academy — a platform built on the belief that every person has the power to shine their light and connect with confidence.

With a career spanning over 35 countries, Darshika has built and led programmes, communities, and initiatives that sit at the intersection of human connection and professional growth. She is a graduate of the Oxford Executive Leadership Programme.

As a nominee for Leader of the Year 2025, and with deep roots in education through partnerships with institutions like Brunel Business School and Northwood College, Darshika brings both credibility and warmth to everything she delivers.

The H! Career Accelerator is a direct expression of her life's work — helping people move from doubt to direction, preparation to confidence, and conversation to real opportunity.
